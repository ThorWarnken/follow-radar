# Flock Business Video Ad — Voiceover Script
**Total runtime: 38 seconds**

---

## Scene 1: Hook (0:00–0:05)
> The average business posts eighteen times a month. Only six of those reach more than ten percent of their followers. The rest? Wasted effort.

**Tone:** Matter-of-fact, slightly provocative. Pause briefly after "followers."

---

## Scene 2: The Ghost Problem (0:05–0:13)
> Here's why. A third of your audience isn't real. Eighteen percent are bots and ghosts. Forty-four percent have gone completely inactive. Less than four in ten will ever see what you post.

**Tone:** Revealing, steady. Let the numbers land.

---

## Scene 3: The Format Trap (0:13–0:21)
> And what you post matters just as much. Static images are dying — down seventeen percent year over year. Meanwhile, Reels are driving forty-seven percent faster growth. The format you choose decides your reach before you write a single word.

**Tone:** Urgent but informative. Speed up slightly on "Reels are driving..."

---

## Scene 4: The Revenue Gap (0:21–0:30)
> Let's do the math. Ten thousand followers. Only thirty-seven percent are real and engaged. That's thirty-seven hundred actual reach. But optimized with the right timing, the right format, and ghost removal? You're looking at two-point-four-x reach and three-point-one-x engagement. That's the difference between a two hundred and a six hundred dollar sponsored post.

**Tone:** Calculated, building momentum. Emphasize "six hundred dollar" with weight.

---

## Scene 5: How It Works (0:30–0:35)
> Flock makes it simple. Install a bookmarklet. Visit Instagram. Get your AI-powered growth report in seconds. No password. No app. No data stored.

**Tone:** Clean, fast, confident. Staccato delivery on the last three phrases.

---

## Scene 6: CTA (0:35–0:38)
> Stop guessing. Start growing.

**Tone:** Bold, final. Let it breathe.

---

# Production Notes

**Voice profile (for ElevenLabs):**
- Male or female, 25-35 age range
- American English, neutral accent
- Confident, warm, conversational — NOT announcer/radio voice
- Moderate pace (~150 words/min)
- Suggested ElevenLabs voices: "Josh" (male, warm), "Rachel" (female, clear), or "Adam" (male, deep confident)

**Total word count:** ~195 words across 38 seconds = ~308 wpm reading pace
- This is fast. If TTS output runs long, cut these lines first:
  - Scene 4: Remove "That's thirty-seven hundred actual reach."
  - Scene 3: Remove "The format you choose decides your reach before you write a single word." (the visual makes the point)

**Music pairing notes:**
- Music should be low during voiceover, swell during transitions between scenes
- Scene 6 (CTA): music hits peak, voiceover stops, let the tagline and music carry the final 3 seconds
